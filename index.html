Downloads "golf_rounds.csv" (a summary of every round) plus one CSV per round with its hole-by-hole details, named "date_GolfClub.csv". The most recent round's file downloads right after the summary. Your browser may ask permission to download multiple files. On phones that support it, "Share CSV Files" opens your device's share sheet so you can send the files straight to Mail (or any app) without downloading them first.
